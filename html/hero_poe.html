Resistance Hero Poe

Offensive Attacker who overpowers his enemies with debilitating effects


Light Side Attacker Resistance


Basic Attack: Flinching Shot

Deal Special damage to target enemy. If Resistance Hero Poe is Inspired, the target enemy becomes Vulnerable for 1 turn.

Vulnerable: Will be critically hit if able

Special Attack : Optic Onslaught (cooldown: 3)

Deal Special damage to target enemy and inflict Daze and Stagger for 2 turns. If Resistance Hero Poe is Inspired he also inflicts 3 Damage Over Time debuffs for 1 turn.

Special Attack : Blaster Bravado (cooldown: 3)

Deal Special damage to target enemy and Stun them for 2 turns. If Resistance Hero Poe is Inspired, dispel all buffs on them and inflict Buff Immunity for 2 turns. This ability can't be evaded.

Unique Ability : Spark of Resistance

If all of Resistance Hero Poe's allies are Light Side, enemies lose 5% Protection whenever they lose a debuff. When Inspired allies deal damage to an enemy with an ability, they also deal bonus damage equal to 10% of the target's Max Health, which can't be evaded. If an enemy critically hits an Inspired ally, all buffs are dispelled from that enemy at the end of the turn.

If Resistance Hero Poe is Inspired when he uses a Special ability during his turn, he can Inspire another target Resistance ally.
