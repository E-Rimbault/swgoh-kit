Sergeant Melshi

A shadowy Rogue One operative who Stealths between strikes, leaving enemies Exposed and helping allies with Spark of Rebellion


Light Side Attacker Rebel Rebel Fighter Rogue One


Basic Attack: Tactical Exploit

Deal Physical damage to target enemy and Expose them for 1 turn. If target enemy has Offense Down, this attack has +50% Defense Penetration. On a critical hit, remove 5% Turn Meter from target enemy.

Sergeant Melshi gains Defense Penetration Up for 2 turns.

Special Attack : Draw Them Out (cooldown: 4)

Inflict Burning on all enemies for 2 turns, and the target enemy is Staggered for 1 turn. Then deal Physical damage to all enemies, dealing 20% more damage for each debuffed enemy.

Allies with Spark of Rebellion gain Tenacity Up for 2 turns.

Special Attack : 10 Men Feel Like 100 (cooldown: 3)

Dispel all buffs on target enemy. Call target other Rogue One ally to assist, and deal Physical damage to target enemy 3 times. The damage from this ability is increased by 5% for each ally with Spark of Rebellion.

Sergeant Melshi gains 10% Critical Chance (stacking, max 100%) and Offense (stacking, max 100%) until the end of encounter for each buff dispelled by this ability.

Unique Ability : Shadow Operations

At the start of each encounter, Rogue One allies gain Spark of Rebellion for 3 turns. Sergeant Melshi Stealths for 1 turn whenever he scores a critical hit during his turn.

If the ally in the Leader slot is Rogue One:
- Whenever Rogue One allies gain Protection Up, they gain 25% Critical Damage (stacking) until the end of their next turn
- Whenever Rogue One allies attack out of turn Sergeant Melshi assists (once per turn), if he can't assist this way, reduce his cooldowns by 1
- Whenever Sergeant Melshi critically hits a targeted enemy that is Dazed, he gains 50% Turn Meter

Omicron Boost : While in Territory Wars and there are no Galactic Legends and all allies are Rogue One: At the start of battle, allies gain 50% Max Health and Max Protection. Allies with Spark of Rebellion are immune to Ability Block and Turn Meter reduction.

Whenever an enemy resists a debuff from an ally, they lose 50% Critical Chance (stacking) and 50% Tenacity (stacking) for 1 turn.

Whenever Sergeant Melshi assists another Rogue One ally during their turn, he deals 50% more damage. While Sergeant Melshi is Stealthed, enemies he defeats can't be revived.
