B2 Super Battle Droid

Droid Tank that relentlessly punishes enemies that evade attacks or damage allies


Dark Side Tank Droïd Separatist


Basic Attack: Heavy Arms

Deal Physical damage to target enemy. If target was Target Locked, inflict Critical Damage Down for 2 turns. Otherwise, inflict Target Lock for 2 turns.

Special Attack : Mow Down (cooldown: 3)

Deal Physical damage to all enemies and dispel all positive status effects on them, with an 80% chance to also inflict Buff Immunity for 2 turns (applied before damage).

Unique Ability : Relentless Barrage

B2 has a 40% chance to gain 100% Turn Meter whenever another ally is evaded or damaged by an attack. Target Locked enemies have -25% Critical Chance and -25% Evasion.

Unique Ability : Reactive Protocol

B2 has a 40% chance to reduce its cooldowns by 1 whenever a Separatist ally is evaded or damaged by an attack. Whenever B2 inflicts a debuff, all other Separatist Droid allies gain 5% Turn Meter.
