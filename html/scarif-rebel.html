Scarif Rebel Pathfinder


Light Side Tank Rebel Rebel Fighter Rogue One Crew Members

Ship: Bistan's U-wing



Basic Attack: Dig In

Deal Physical damage to target enemy and gain Offense Up for 2 turns. Grant Accuracy Up for 2 turns to a random ally who doesn't have it.

Special Attack : Explosive Entry (cooldown: 3)

Deal Physical damage to all enemies with a 70% chance to inflict Offense Down for 3 turns. Additionally, gain Taunt for 2 turns.

Unique Ability : Do or Die

If Scarif Rebel Pathfinder has any buffs when he is defeated, he is revived with 15% Max Health and 20% Turn Meter for each living Rebel ally. Whenever Scarif Rebel Pathfinder is defeated all other allies gain Speed Up for 2 turns.
