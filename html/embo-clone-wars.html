Embo

Deadly Bounty Hunter Attacker that consumes his Protection to boost his damage


Dark Side Attacker Leader Bounty Hunter Hutt Cartel Mercenary Scoundrel


Basic Attack: Dismantling Shot

Dispel all buffs and deal Physical damage to target enemy.

Special Attack : Kyuzo War Helmet (cooldown: 3)

Deal Physical damage to target enemy. This attack deals bonus damage equal to 35% of Embo's Max Protection. Then, Embo loses 50% Protection.

Leader Ability: The Quiet Assassin

All Bounty Hunters gain +50% Counter Chance and +50% Health Steal. When a Bounty Hunter ally scores a Critical Hit during their turn, they gain Offense Up for 1 turn. When Embo is in the Leader slot, and not the Ally slot, the following Contract is active:

Contract: Damage an enemy without buffs 15 times (Only Bounty Hunter allies can contribute to the Contract.)

Reward: All Bounty Hunter allies gain +50% Offense. When a Bounty Hunter ally uses a basic ability, they deal bonus damage equal to 7% of the target's Max Protection.

Unique Ability : Way of the Kyuzo

Embo can't be critically hit. While Embo has Protection he gains +30% Offense, while Embo has no Protection he gains +30% Critical Chance. When Embo receives damage from an enemy's attack, if he has no Protection, he gains Foresight for 2 turns.

Omicron Boost : While in Territory Wars: Bounty Hunter allies gain +40% Max Protection. At the end of Embo's turn, Bounty Hunter allies recover Protection equal to 25% of Embo's Max Protection, doubled for Embo. While Embo has Protection, all Bounty Hunter allies gain +30% Defense and 5% Turn Meter when a Bounty Hunter ally receives damage while debuffed.

Embo's Payout

Whenever Embo receives Rewards from a Contract, he also gains the following Payout. (Contracts are granted by certain Bounty Hunter Leader Abilities.)

Payout: Embo takes a bonus turn after scoring a Critical Hit. Embo can't take consecutive bonus turns. Embo gains a new Special ability "Elusive Strikes".

Elusive Strikes: Deal Physical damage and recover Protection equal to the damage dealt. This attack ignores Protection.

Elusive Strikes (cooldown : 4)

Deal Physical damage and recover Protection equal to the damage dealt. This attack ignores Protection.
