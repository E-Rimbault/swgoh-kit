Garazeb "Zeb" Orrelios

Versatile Phoenix Tank with wide variety of effects for allies or enemies with or without debuffs


Light Side Tank Rebel Phoenix Crew Members

Ship: Ghost



Basic Attack: Bash

Deal Physical damage to target enemy and Daze them for 2 turns. If that enemy was already Dazed, Exposed, or Staggered, they are Stunned for 1 turn instead. This attack deals 30% more damage to debuffed enemies.

Special Attack : Staggering Sweep (cooldown: 3)

Deal Physical damage to target enemy and Stagger them for 1 turn. Zeb gains 10% Turn Meter for each active Phoenix ally. If the target has no debuffs, this attack can't be resisted, deals double damage, and deals bonus damage equal to 20% of the enemy's Max Health. If the target has any buffs, ignore their Armor. If the target has any debuffs, this attack has +50% Critical Damage. This attack can't be evaded.

Special Attack : Honor Guard (cooldown: 4)

Zeb and target ally gain Protection Up (50%) for 2 turns. If Zeb or target ally is debuffed, they gain Retribution for 2 turns, otherwise they gain Tenacity Up for 2 turns.

Unique Ability : Shrug Off

Zeb has bonus Max Protection equal to +40% of his Max Health and recovers 15% Protection at the start of his turn. This recovery is doubled if Zeb is debuffed.
