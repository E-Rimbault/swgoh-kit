shoretrooper

Empire Tank who enters battle with Taunt, heals allies, and grants them Critical Hit Immunity


Dark side Tank Empire Empire Trooper Crew Members

ship: TIE Reaper



Basic Attack: Target Weapons

Deal Physical damage to target enemy with a 70% chance to inflict Critical Chance Down for 3 turns.

special Attack : Regroup (cooldown: 4)

All allies recover Health equal to 24% of shoretrooper's Max Health and gain Critical Hit Immunity for 2 turns. shoretrooper gains Taunt for 2 turns.

Whenever shoretrooper loses a buff, reduce the cooldown of this ability by 1.

Unique Ability : stand Guard

At the start of each encounter shoretrooper gains Taunt for 1 turn.

Whenever another allied Empire unit uses a special ability, if shoretrooper is Taunting, they gain 15% Turn Meter and shoretrooper recovers 10% Max Health.

While shoretrooper is active, Imperial Trooper allies have +10% Max Health.
