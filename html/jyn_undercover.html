Jyn Erso (Undercover)

A Rogue One Attacker who goes undercover on Scarif to infiltrate the data vault within


Light Side Attacker Rebelle Rebel Fighter Rogue One


Basic Attack: I Rebel

Deal Physical damage and inflict Breach on the target enemy for 1 turn and remove 10% Turn Meter. If the target enemy was already Breached, Jyn Erso (Undercover) attacks again and deals 20% more damage.

Special Attack : Get This Over With (cooldown: 3)

Remove 100% Turn Meter from the target enemy. If target enemy is Stunned, Jyn assists.

If a Rogue One ally is Taunting, the next time she deals damage, she deals 10% more damage for each stack of Expose on all enemies and target ally gains Critical Avoidance Up for 2 turns. If target ally is a Rogue One Tank, they also gain Defense Up and Protection Up (50%, stacking) for 2 turns.

Omicron Boost : While in Grand Arenas: Remove 100% Turn Meter from all enemies inflicted with Breach.

Special Attack : We Have Hope (cooldown: 4)

Deal Physical damage to target enemy and gain 25% Offense (stacking, max 200%) until the end of battle. If Jyn has Spark of Rebellion, she dispels it, takes a bonus turn, and resets all other Rogue One allies' cooldowns.

Unique Ability : Rebellions are Built on Hope

At the start of battle, Jyn has +10% Tenacity for each Rogue One ally. If all allies are Rogue One, they gain 40% Max Health and Max Protection.

If all allies are Rogue One, whenever an ally is Dazed, they dispel it and recover 100% Protection and whenever an ally attacks out of turn, they gain 20% Defense (stacking, max 200%).

Whenever a Rogue One Tank ally uses an ability, they gain Accuracy Up for 1 turn.

Omicron Boost : While in Grand Arenas: At the start of battle, if all allies are Rogue One, allies take 20% less damage.

Whenever an enemy has been inflicted with debuffs 10 times, starting on the next turn, the next time they are damaged, that enemy takes 50% more damage.

Whenever Jyn is Dazed, Distracted, Shocked, or Stunned, all non-Tank Rogue One allies gain Speed Up for 2 turns, and at the end of that turn, she Stuns the enemy that inflicted the debuff for 2 turns.

Whenever Empire enemies with less than 100% Turn Meter gain bonus Turn Meter, they can't gain bonus Turn Meter until the start of their next turn.

Unique Ability : Time To Fight Is Now

Whenever Jyn triggers Expose while she has Spark of Rebellion, she gains 10% Critical Chance and Critical Damage (stacking, max 200%).

Whenever Jyn gains Spark of Rebellion:

- Dispel all debuffs from herself
- Inflict all enemies with Breach and Buff Disruption for 2 turns, and Healing Immunity for 1 turn

Jyn gains 20 Speed (max 100) for every 5 times she gains Spark of Rebellion.

Omicron Boost : While in Grand Arenas: Whenever she loses Spark of Rebellion, her cooldowns are reduced by 1.

The first time she gains Spark of Rebellion from another ally, she dispels it from herself.

Whenever Jyn gains a bonus turn, she dispels Spark of Rebellion from herself the next time she uses I Rebel.
