R5-D56


Dark Side Attacker Sith Droïd

Original Unit : The Ronin


Basic Attack: Arc Jab

Deal Physical damage to target enemy and Shock them for 1 turn. The Ronin gains Protection Up (50%) for 1 turn. If the target enemy has Bleed, this attack deals 100% more damage.

Special Attack : (cooldown: 3)

Deal Physical damage to all enemies. Deal an additional instance of damage for each enemy (max 6) with Resilient Defense, Stealth, or Taunt. R5-D56 Siphons Critical Chance from the target enemy, which can't be resisted, and The Ronin gains the same amount. The Ronin has +50 Speed for the rest of the encounter. Inflict a stack of Bleed (max 6) until the end of the encounter, which can't be dispelled or resisted if the enemy has Resilient Defense, Stealth, or Taunt.

Unique Ability : Follow the Blade

R5-D56 gains 20% Max Health and Protection for each Sith ally. Whenever The Ronin starts his turn, R5-D56 gains a bonus turn. On summon, R5-D56 gains Crit Chance Up and Defense Penetration Up for 2 turns.

Whenever an enemy gains bonus Turn Meter, he gains 1 stack of Boil Over (max 10) which can't be copied, dispelled, or prevented until the end of the encounter. R5-D56 gains 10% Offense per stack of Boil Over. When R5-D56 reaches 10 stacks of Boil Over, The Ronin gains a Bonus turn and dispels all stacks of Boil Over at the start of that turn.

Unique Ability : Summoned

- This unit's stats scale with the summoner's stats.
- This unit can only be summoned to the ally slot if it's available.
- This unit can't be summoned in certain raids.
- If an effect counts defeated units, this unit doesn't count.
- When there are no other allied combatants, this unit escapes from battle.
- A unit can't be revived if this summoned unit exists in their slot.
