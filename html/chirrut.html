Chirrut Îmwe

Balanced Rebel Attacker who dispels debuffs, heals allies, and grants Tenacity Up


Light Side Attacker Rebelle Rebel Fighter Rogue One


Basic Attack: Staff Strike

Deal Physical damage to target enemy with a 70% chance to gain Heal Over Time for 3 turns. If the target is debuffed, grant Heal Over Time to a random ally for 3 turns.

Special Attack : As the Force Wills (cooldown: 4)

All allies have their current Health percentages equalized. Dispel all debuffs from all allies and grant them a Heal Over Time effect for 4 turns for each effect dispelled. Allies that were not debuffed gain Tenacity Up for 3 turns.

Special Attack : Strength of Purpose (cooldown: 3)

Deal Physical damage to target enemy, dealing 20% more damage for each buffed ally. If Baze Malbus is present, he is called to Assist.

Unique Ability : Indomitable Will

Chirrut Îmwe gains +17% Counter Chance and Health Steal for each living enemy, doubled for Empire enemies. If Baze Malbus is present he also gains these bonuses.

Unique Ability : Resolute Endurance

Whenever a Rebel ally is Critically Hit they gain Heal Over Time for 2 turns. At the start of each turn, if Chirrut Îmwe is alive, all allies with Heal Over Time recover 4% of their Max Health.
