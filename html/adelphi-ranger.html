Adelphi Ranger

Increases Deflection Chance, Dodge Chance, Health, and Protection.


Light Side Tank New Republic Rebel Crew Members


Basic Attack: Practiced Shot

Deal Physical damage to target enemy and inflict them with Defense Down for 1 turn.

During Adelphi Ranger's turn, gain 1 stack of Bulwark for 1 turn (limit once per turn). During enemy turns, Daze the target enemy for 1 turn at the end of this turn (limit once per turn).

Special Attack : Sentinel's Strike (cooldown: 3)

Adelphi Ranger Taunts for 2 turns and all New Republic allies assist dealing 50% less damage.

If Captain Carson Teva is the ally in the Leader slot, the assists deal 10% less damage instead and Adelphi Ranger recovers 25% Protection, doubled if she was Taunting at the start of this turn.

Special Attack : Adelphi Cavalry (cooldown: 4)

Dispel all buffs on all enemies. Deal Physical damage to all enemies and inflict them with 1 stack of On the Run until the end of the encounter, which can't be copied, dispelled, or resisted. All New Republic allies gain 3 stacks of Bulwark for 3 turns.

Unique Ability : Rodian Ranger

At the start of battle if Captain Carson Teva is the ally in the Leader slot, gain the following effects:

- If there are no Galactic Legends, Teva can't be defeated while Adelphi Ranger is Taunting
- Whenever a New Republic ally gains Retribution, they gain Riposte for 2 turns
- Whenever a New Republic ally counterattacks, if they have fewer than 10 stacks of Bulwark they gain 1 stack of Bulwark for 2 turns
- If there are no Galactic Legends, Adelphi Ranger Taunts for 2 turns at the start of each encounter, which can't be dispelled or prevented

At the start of each encounter, all New Republic allies gain 15% Max Health and Max Protection (stacking) for each active New Republic ally and 20% Defense (stacking) for each active Droid ally until they are defeated, doubled for Adelphi Ranger.

If there are no allied Galactic Legends, whenever a Droid ally Taunts, dispel it and they gain Outmaneuver for 2 turns. Then, Adelphi Ranger gains 5 stacks of Bulwark and Taunts for 2 turns.

The first time Adelphi Ranger gains any stacks of Bulwark each turn, Teva gains 1 stack of Bulwark for 1 turn.

If there are no Galactic Legends, whenever enemies take consecutive turns, all New Republic allies who have fewer than 10 stacks of Bulwark gain 2 stacks of Bulwark for 1 turn.

While on Defense and there are no Galactic Legends, whenever a New Republic ally takes damage before Adelphi Ranger's first turn, they dispel their debuffs, gain Damage Immunity until the end of that turn, and all New Republic allies gain 5% Defense and Offense per active Droid ally (stacking) for the rest of battle. If C-3PO is an ally, these stat increases are doubled and Stealthed enemies are Ability Blocked for 1 turn, which can't be dispelled, evaded, or resisted.

Omicron Boost : While in Grand Arenas, if all allies are New Republic and there are no Galactic Legends: At the start of battle, New Republic allies gain 40% Max Health and they also gain 40% Max Protection for each active New Republic ally until the first time they are defeated.

Whenever Adelphi Ranger Taunts, loses Taunt, or uses Adelphi Cavalry, all allies recover 50% Health and Protection.

If R5-D4 is an ally, whenever another ally takes damage while Adelphi Ranger is Taunting the cooldown for Bad Motivator is reduced by 2. When R5-D4 uses Bad Motivator, he inflicts all enemies with Healing Immunity for 2 turns, inflicts Support and Healer enemies with Marked for 2 turns, and inflicts the weakest enemy with 9 stacks of On the Run (stacking, max 10) until the end of the encounter. These effects can't be dispelled, evaded, or resisted.

While on Defense and if C-3PO is an ally, whenever an ally takes damage before Adelphi Ranger's first turn, all allies gain an additional 20% Defense and Offense per active Droid ally (stacking) for the rest of battle and non-Tank allies have their cooldowns decreased by 2. At the end of Adelphi Ranger's first turn, all allies gain 30 Speed for the rest of battle.
