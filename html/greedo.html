Greedo

Deadly Attacker that gains extra attacks from Critical Hits


Dark Side Attacker leader Bounty Hunter Hutt Cartel Scoundrel


Basic Attack: Who Shot First?

Deal Physical damage to target enemy with a 70% chance to gain Potency Up for 1 turn. When attacking an enemy with a Thermal Detonator on them, this attack has +25% Critical Damage.

Special Attack : Explosive Sabotage (cooldown: 5)

Place Thermal Detonators on all enemies. After 1 turn, these Thermal Detonators explode and Greedo gains 25% Turn Meter for each Thermal Detonator that explodes.

Special Attack : Threaten (cooldown: 4)

All allies gain Critical Chance Up for 2 turns. Reduce target enemy's Turn Meter by an amount equal to Greedo's Critical Chance, which can't be resisted (excludes raid bosses and Galactic Legends). Then, Greedo gains an equal amount of Turn Meter removed this way. If the target is reduced to less than 20% Turn Meter, Stun them for 1 turn, which can't be resisted. Dispel all buffs on target enemy. This attack can't be evaded.

Leader Ability: Ambuscade

Scoundrel allies gain 50% Critical Damage. When Greedo is in the Leader slot, and not the Ally slot, the following Contract is active:

Contract: Damage an enemy with less than 50% Turn Meter 10 times with a Basic Ability. (Only Bounty Hunter allies can contribute to the Contract.)

Reward: All Bounty Hunter allies have +50% Critical Damage for the rest of the battle and their Payouts are activated.

Unique Ability : If at First You Don't Succeed

Greedo has +15% Critical Chance and a 50% chance to attack again whenever he scores a Critical Hit (no cap).

Greedo's Payout

Whenever Greedo receives a Reward from a Contract, he also gains the following Payout. (Contracts are granted by certain Bounty Hunter Leader Abilities.)

Payout: Greedo has +75% Counter chance, +25% Critical Damage, and +50% Health Steal until the end of battle.
