Bail Organa

A steadfast Tank who bears the Empire's gaze while quietly protecting the future of the Rebellion


Light Side Tank Rebel


Basic Attack: Quiet Defiance

Inflict Offense Down on target enemy for 1 turn. If it is Bail Organa's turn and the ally in the Leader slot is a Rebel, also remove 10% Turn Meter from target enemy.

Special Attack : Political Cover (cooldown: 3)

Gain 8 stacks of Resilient Defense (max 10) until the end of the encounter. Bail Organa and targeted ally gain Defense Up for 2 turns.

If the targeted ally is a Rebel Fighter, reduce their cooldowns by 1 and they and Bail Organa gain Diplomatic Immunity for 2 turns, which can't be copied.

Diplomatic Immunity: Can't be instantly defeated; if defeated, revives with 100% Health; if dispelled, gain 20% Turn Meter and Damage Immunity for 1 turn.

Special Attack : Alderaanian Resolve (cooldown: 3)

Rebel allies recover 25% Protection and gain Protection Up (20%, stacking) for 2 turns.

All non-tank Rebel Fighter allies Stealth and all Rebel Fighters gain Tenacity Up for 2 turns.

Rally Bail Organa, Mon Mothma, and all Rebel Fighter allies.

Rallied characters act based on their role:

- Attackers assist
- Healers and Supports restore 15% Health and Protection to all allies
- Tanks Taunt and gain Critical Hit Immunity for 2 turns

Allies that were Rallied this way also gain the following benefits based on their role:

- Attackers gain Diplomatic Immunity for 2 turns
- Healers and Supports gain 25% Turn Meter
- Tanks Gain 5 stacks of Resilient Defense (max 10) until the end of the encounter

Unique Ability : Hope for a Better Dawn

At the start of each encounter, Bail Organa gains 6 stacks of Resilient Defense (max 10) until the end of battle.

Whenever Bail Organa Taunts, he dispels it and gains 5 stacks of Resilient Defense (max 10) until the end of the encounter.

If there are no other allied combatants at the start of a turn, Bail escapes from battle.

If Mon Mothma is the ally in the Leader slot, Bail Organa gains the Rebel Fighter tag for the rest of the battle.

Whenever a Rebel Fighter ally with Diplomatic Immunity attacks out of turn, Bail Organa gains 2% Defense (stacking, max 100%) until the end of the encounter.

If there are no Galactic Legend allies: Whenever Bail Organa gains Resilient Defense, Rebel Fighter allies gain 10% Turn Meter (once per turn). Whenever Resilient Defense is dispelled, Bail Organa gains 5 stacks of Resilient Defense (max 10) until the end of the encounter.

Omicron boost : While in Grand Arenas and there are no Galactic Legends: At the start of battle, Rebel Fighter allies gain Protection Up (50%) for 2 turns.

The first time each Rebel Fighter ally is reduced to 50% Health or less, Bail Organa gains 5 stacks of Resilient Defense (max 10) until the end of battle, that Rebel Fighter ally Stealths for 2 turns, and recovers 50% Health and Protection.

Whenever Bail Organa gains a stack of Resilient Defense, Rebel Fighter allies gain 10% Defense (stacking, max 100%) and 15% Offense (stacking, max 225%) until the end of battle.

Once per turn, whenever another Rebel Fighter ally gains Turn Meter, Bail Organa gains 10% Turn Meter.
