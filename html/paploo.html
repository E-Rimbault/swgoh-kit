Paploo

Increases Armor, Health Steal, and Protection.


Light Side Tank Ewok


Basic Attack: Vigilant Strike

Deal Physical damage and Dispel all buffs from target enemy. Paploo gains Defense Up for 2 turns. If an Ewok ally calls Paploo to Assist, they also gain Defense Up for 2 turns.

Special Attack : Diversion (cooldown: 3)

Deal Physical damage to target enemy, inflict Speed Down for 2 turns, and Paploo gains Taunt for 2 turns.

Special Attack : Galvanize (cooldown: 4)

Dispel all debuffs from Paploo and target other ally. If the target other ally is an Ewok, they are called to Assist and gain 10% Turn Meter.

Unique Ability : Don't Hold Back

Whenever Paploo gains a status effect, he recovers 5% Health and Protection. Whenever Paploo gains Stealth, dispel Stealth and gain Taunt for 2 Turns. Paploo has +25% Speed whenever he does not have Taunt active.
