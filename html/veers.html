General Veers

Aggressive Empire Support that becomes stronger as enemies are defeated


Dark Side Support Leader Empire Empire Trooper


Basic Attack: Daring Attack

Deal Physical damage to target enemy with a 70% chance each to grant Speed Up for 2 turns to General Veers and a random Imperial Trooper ally who doesn't have it.

Special Attack : Ruthless Assault (cooldown: 4)

Deal Special damage to all enemies with a 55% chance to inflict Ability Block for 1 turn, dealing 10% more damage for each living Rebel enemy. Call all other Imperial Trooper allies to assist, dealing 40% less damage.

Leader Ability: Imperial Assault Commander

Empire allies gain 30% Offense. Imperial Trooper allies gain 20 Speed and gain 10% Turn Meter whenever they gain a buff.

Unique Ability : Aggressive Tactician

Whenever an enemy is defeated while Veers is active, Imperial Trooper allies gain Offense Up for 2 turns, gain 50% Turn Meter, and recover 10% Protection.

While Veers is active, Imperial Trooper allies have +15% Critical Chance.
