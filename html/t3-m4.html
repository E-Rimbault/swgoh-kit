T3-M4

Offensive Droid who supports a Droid team with extra stats and debuffs the enemy team


Light Side Support Droïd Old Republic Crew Members

Ship: Ebon Hawk



Basic Attack: Disable

Deal Special damage to target enemy with a 75% chance to inflict Ability Block for 1 turn. If the enemy has Target Lock, the Ability Block can't be Resisted.

Special Attack : Repair Kit (cooldown: 3)

Dispel all debuffs from target ally. If that ally was a Droid, also recover 50% Health, 50% Protection, and gain Critical Hit Immunity and Offense Up for 2 turns.

Special Attack : Carbonite Projector (cooldown: 4)

Dispel all buffs on all enemies and deal Special damage to them. Inflict Critical Chance Down and Offense Down for 2 turns, then remove 20% Turn Meter from all enemies. Target Locked enemies can't resist these debuffs.

Unique Ability : Combat Logic Upgrade

Droid allies gain 8% Potency and 8% Critical Hit Chance (stacking) each time T3-M4 uses an ability. Also, Droid allies have +8% Critical Damage for each debuff on the enemy team.

Unique Ability : Master Gearhead

T3-M4 has +100% Defense Penetration, and Droid allies gain 100% of T3-M4's Defense Penetration. Additionally, each time T3-M4 starts his turn, he has a 75% chance to inflict Target Lock for 2 turns on a random enemy who does not already have it. This Target Lock can't be evaded.

Omicron Boost : While in Territory Wars: At the start of the encounter, T3-M4 gains Stealth for 2 turns, and Droid allies gain 15 Armor Penetration and 30% Max Health. Droid allies have +20% Defense for each Target Locked enemy, +20% Defense while debuffed, and +20% Defense while buffed. At the start of T3-M4's turn, inflict Target Lock for 2 turns on a random enemy who does not already have it, which can't be evaded. Then, all allies recover 5% Health and Protection for each Target Locked enemy.
