K-2SO

Rebel Tank who Taunts and Counter Attacks


Light Side Tank Droïd Rebel Rebel Fighter Rogue One Crew Members

Ship: Cassian's U-WING



Basic Attack: Carboplast Composite Fist

Deal Physical damage to target enemy with a 70% chance to inflict Offense Down for 2 turns.

Special Attack : Conductive Charge (cooldown: 2)

Deal Physical damage to target enemy and Daze them for 2 turns. If Cassian Andor is present, he is called to Assist.

Unique Ability : Enforcer

Whenever K-2SO uses an abilty, he has a 75% (100% while buffed) chance to Taunt for 1 turn. This Taunt is dispelled whenever K-2SO takes damage.

Unique Ability : Reprogrammed Imperial Droid

K-2SO has +97.6% Counter Chance. This chance is halved while K-2SO is debuffed. In addition, K-2SO gains 1% Max Protection whenever he takes damage.
