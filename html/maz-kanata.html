Maz Kanata

A loyal Support to her pirate crew; feared, respected, and known by many as the Pirate Queen


Light Side Support Leader Pirate Scoundrel Smuggler Unaligned Force User


Basic Attack: Steadfast Shot

Deal Special damage to target enemy and inflict them with Healing Immunity and Pinned for 1 turn. If target enemy is Sith, Healing Immunity and Pinned can't be evaded or resisted.

Special Attack : Eyes Everywhere (cooldown: 5)

Dispel all debuffs on all Pirate and Smuggler allies.

If all allies were Pirates and if there were no Galactic Legend allies at the start of battle, call all Pirate allies to assist, dealing 40% less damage, and they gain 50% Turn Meter and have their cooldowns reduced by 1.

Special Attack : Keeper of Secrets (cooldown: 4)

Maz Steals Stealth, resets its duration, and grants it to all non-Tank Pirate allies and she Steals 1 stack of Profit that she granted to enemies from each enemy until the end of the encounter.

If all allies are Pirates and there are no Galactic Legend allies at the start of battle, inflict all enemies with 3 stacks of Expose for 2 turns and the target enemy's cooldowns are increased by 1.

This attack can't be evaded or resisted.

Omicron Boost : While in Grand Arenas and there are no Galactic Legend allies: If all allies were Pirates at the start of battle, Maz Steals an additional stack of Profit that she granted to enemies from each enemy until the end of encounter, and all enemies have their cooldowns increased by 1 instead.

If target enemy had lost all 5 stacks of Profit given from Maz, Deathmark them until the end of encounter, which can't be copied or dispelled. If the enemy already had Deathmark, set target enemy to 100 Max Health until the end of encounter. If target enemy was a Galactic Legend, inflict them with Armor Shred instead, which can't be dispelled, until the end of encounter.

Leader Ability: Pirate Queen

At the start of each encounter and if there were no Galactic Legend allies:

- All Pirate and Smuggler allies are immune to Fear and they gain Foresight for 1 turn
- Whenever Maz gains a stack of Profit, all other Pirate allies gain 1 stack of Profit until the end of the encounter
- Whenever a Pirate ally with Retribution uses a Basic ability, they ignore that enemy's Protection - Whenever an enemy uses a Basic ability, all Pirate allies gain 5% Turn Meter
- If there were no Galactic Legend enemies at the start of battle: If there is an enemy Sith, Pirate allies take reduced damage from percent Health effects, and whenever they target an enemy Sith with an ability, they deal 300% more damage and ignore Protection, and at the start of each Sith enemy's turn, all Pirate allies gain Foresight for 1 turn

Omicron Boost : While in Grand Arenas and there are no Galactic Legend allies: Pirate and Smuggler allies gain 30% Max Health and Max Protection, and 30 Speed.

Whenever a Pirate ally scores a critical hit, they gain 5% Tenacity (stacking) until the end of encounter.

If a Pirate ally is instantly defeated by a non-Galactic Legend enemy, that enemy is Deathmarked for 1 turn, which can't be copied, dispelled, or prevented. If a Pirate ally uses an ability on their turn against an enemy with this Deathmark, they instantly defeat them.

The condition of no Galactic Legend enemies at the start of battle is removed on this ability.

Unique Ability : Ancient Wisdom

The maximum cap for Profit is removed for Maz Kanata.

At the start of battle and if all allies were Pirates, Maz gains 100% counter chance and 75% Critical Chance until the end of battle, and 20% Max Health and Max Protection until the first time she is defeated.

If there were no Galactic Legend Pirate allies or enemies at the start of battle, at the start of Maz's first turn each encounter:

- Grant all non-Pirate enemies 5 Stacks of Profit, which can't be prevented, until the end of encounter
- Whenever Maz critically hits an enemy, she Steals a stack of Profit from that enemy until the end of the encounter
- Whenever an enemy loses all 5 stacks of Profit, Maz gains a bonus turn

omicron Boost : While in Grand Arenas and there are no Galactic Legend allies: Maz gains 5% Mastery whenever she gains a stack of Profit until the end of battle.

Whenever an enemy loses a stack of Profit given from Maz, they also lose 5% Offense (stacking, max 25%) and 5 Speed (stacking, max 25) until the end of battle.

Whenever a Pirate ally inflicts a debuff on their turn, they deal True damage to all enemies, once per turn (this damage can't defeat them).
