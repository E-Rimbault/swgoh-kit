Story & Rules

STORY+
The Gotra Boss Guards stand as an unyielding last line of defense protecting Hutt Palace. Those who seek the palace's inner sanctum must overcome these relentless enforcers guarding the seat of power.
RULES+
• Time Limit: 2d 23h
• Requirements: 4★ · Droid Destruction Raid
• Roster Refresh: Never
• Launch Cost: 180K
• Max Attempts: 5

Faction Bonuses

Characters can obtain one or more of the following abilities during the raid.
Faction Bonus: New Republic
Whenever a Healer or Support ally targets the Gotra Boss Guard (Detonator)'s Core with an ability while it's inflicted with On the Run, dispel all debuffs on all allies, they recover 10% Health and Protection, and they inflict the Core with 3 stacks of Expose for 1 turn. If there is an allied The Mandalorian and Grogu or Rotta the Hutt, inflict the Core with 6 stacks of Expose instead.
Faction Bonus: Sith
Allies gain 40% Defense, Max Health, and Max Protection, until the end of battle. Whenever a Sith ally uses a Special ability while they have Siphon, they gain 15% Critical Damage for 1 turn.
Faction Bonus: Rebel
All non-New Republic allies gain 50% Max Health and Max Protection and 30% Potency until the end of battle. Whenever an enemy is inflicted with Undermine (limit once per turn):

- Dispel all debuffs on all allies
- All allies recover 20% Health and Protection
- Vel gains a stack of Resilient Defense until she is damaged
Faction Bonus: Empire
Allies gain 45% Defense, Max Health, Max Protection, and Offense until the end of battle. Whenever an ally inflicted with Undermined damages any arm, Expose target enemy for 1 turn, and all allies gain 25% Turn Meter.
Hero Bonus: The Mandalorian and Grogu
If The Mandalorian and Grogu is the ally in the Leader slot, and there are four other New Republic allies, or if The Mandalorian and Grogu is the ally in the Leader slot, there are three other New Republic allies, and Yoda & Chewie are allies:

- All allies gain 50% Defense, Max Health and Max Protection until the end of battle and the Wookiee tag
- Yoda & Chewie gain the New Republic tag and all allies gain Master's Training until they are defeated
- The Mandalorian and Grogu is considered Jedi Knight Luke Skywalker and Tarfful
- If Mara Jade Skywalker is an ally, Mara Jade Skywalker, The Mandalorian and Grogu, and Yoda & Chewie gain Jedi's Will until they are defeated
- Whenever a Healer or Support ally uses an ability on Gotra Boss Guard (Detonator)'s Core, call all allies to assist (limit once per turn)
Hero Bonus: Rotta the Hutt
If Carson Teva is the ally in the Leader slot and there are 3 other New Republic allies:

- Rotta gains the New Republic tag, and all other allies gain the Hutt Cartel tag
- Rotta gains 50% Defense until the end of battle
- Whenever Rotta targets Gotra Boss Guard (Detonator)'s Arms with an ability while they are inflicted with Bleed, Rotta gains 30% Turn Meter, all allies recover 30% Health and Protection, and gain 50% Offense for 2 turns
- Whenever any Arms are defeated, Gotra Boss Guard (Detonator)'s Core is inflicted with a stack of On the Run until the end battle, which can't be evaded or resisted
- Whenever an ally attacks Gotra Boss Guard (Detonator)'s Left Arm out of turn, the cooldown of Lob Explosive is no longer decreased
Hero Bonus: Carson Teva
If Carson Teva is the ally in the Leader slot and there are 4 other New Republic allies, or if Carson Teva is the ally in the Leader slot, there are three other New Republic allies, and Rotta the Hutt is an ally:

- All allies gain 40% Defense, Max Health, and Max Protection until the end of battle
- Whenever an ally uses an ability on Gotra Boss Guard (Detonator)'s Core while it's inflicted with On the Run, reduce the cooldown of R5-D4's Bad Motivator by 2, if Rotta is an ally, decrease it by 4 instead
Hero Bonus: Darth Jar Jar
If Darth Jar Jar is the ally in the Leader slot, and there are 3 other Sith allies, and Inquisitor Barriss is an ally:

- Inquisitor Barriss gains the Sith tag
- All allies gain the Unaligned Force User tag
- Gotra Boss Guard (Detonator)'s Core is considered the Shield Generator
- Whenever an enemy is inflicted with Plasma Residue, they are inflicted with 2 stacks of Purge (max 6) until the end of battle, and all enemies Taunt until the end of The Ronin's next turn, which can't be evaded, dispelled, or resisted
- Whenever an enemy Arm is inflicted with Manipulated, they have -80% Critical Chance until the end of battle
Hero Bonus: IG-90
If IG-90 is the ally in the Leader slot, there are 3 other Bounty Hunters allies, and one of the following is true:
- Jaxxon is an ally
- Mercenary Guard Droid (B1) is an ally

Then:

- All allies gain the Scoundrel tag
- All allies gain the Mercenary tag
- Jaxxon gains the Bounty Hunter tag, and all allies gain Prepared at the start of battle
- Mercenary Guard Droid (B1) gains 5 stacks of Mercenary Battalion
- All allies gain Defense Up for 2 turns, which can't be dispelled
- Whenever an ally is critically hit, they gain Foresight for 2 turns and all allies recover 10% Health and Protection and have their debuffs dispelled
Hero Bonus: Cassian Andor (Undercover)
If Cassian Andor (Undercover) is the ally in the Leader slot and there are 4 other Rebel allies:

- Cassian is considered Commander Luke Skywalker and Princess Leia
- Whenever Cassian uses Varian Skye, grant a random ally VIP who doesn't already have it until they are damaged twice and allies gain Call to Action for 1 turn
- If Starkiller (Luke Concept) or Stormtrooper Luke are allies, they gain 55% Defense and Offense for the rest of battle and the Rebel Fighter tag
- At the start of battle, inflict all enemies with Undermine for 1 turn
Hero Bonus: Stormtrooper Luke
If Stormtrooper Luke is the ally in the Leader slot and there are 4 other Rebel allies:

- All allies gain the Rebel Figher tag and 40% Defense, Max Health, and Max Protection

- Whenever an enemy is inflicted with Breach, they are inflicted with Undermine for 2 turns, which can't be evaded or resisted, and a random ally who wasn't already Stealthed, Stealths for 1 turn
- Stealthed allies deal 2 additional instances of True damage
- If Starkiller (Luke Concept) is an ally, he gains 40% Offense until the end of battle, and whenever he inflicts an enemy with Buff Immunity, all allies gain 40% Critical Damage and Advantage for 1 turn
Hero Bonus: Major Partagaz
If Major Partagaz is the ally in the Leader slot, there are two other ISB allies, and one of the following is true:
- There are two Imperial Trooper allies
- There is one Imperial Trooper ally, and there is an allied Maul (Hate-Fueled)

Then: - Major Partagaz ignores Taunt effects for his first turn
- All allies gain the Empire, Imperial Trooper, and ISB tag
- If Maul (Hate-Fueled) is not an ally, all Tanks Taunt for 1 turn, which can't be dispelled

Gotra Boss Guard (Malfunction)'s Head

Gotra Boss Guard (Malfunction)'s Head is immune to Provoked, can't counter attack, Stealth or be reduced below 1% Health, and gains 9000% Critical Avoidance and Defense for each active Gotra Boss Guard (Malfunction)'s Left Arm and Gotra Boss Guard (Malfunction)'s Right Arm.

Whenever Gotra Boss Guard (Malfunction)'s Head uses this granted ability, it revives its Left and Right arms at 100% Health, and the Head is inflicted with a stack of Malfunction and gains Reinitializing. Whenever Gotra Boss Guard (Malfunction)'s Head gains Reinitializing, it dispels all buffs, debuffs (excluding Malfunction), and status effects on itself, and its Left and Right arms can't take turns.

Whenever Reinitializing expires on Gotra Boss Guard (Malfunction)'s Head, it recovers 100% Health, and it revives its Left and Right arms at 100% Health, which can't be prevented. If Gotra Boss Guard (Malfunction)'s Head and Gotra Boss Guard (Detonator)'s Core both had Reinitializing and all arms were defeated, Gotra Boss Guard (Malfunction)'s Head and Gotra Boss Guard (Detonator)'s Core dispel Reinitializing and all arms are revived at 100% Health, which can't be prevented.

Malfunction: -10% Accuracy and Defense; +50 Speed
