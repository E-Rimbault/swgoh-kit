Order 66

Story & Rules

STORY+
Operation Knightfall has commenced and Lord Vader has begun his extermination of the Jedi. Fight through the Dark Side Clone Troopers blocking your path. Attempt to interrupt his onslaught before Order 66 is completed.
RULES+
• Time Limit: 2d 23h
• Requirements: 5 Stars, Specific Units
• Roster Refresh: Never
• Launch Cost: 180K
• Max Attempts: 5

Faction Bonuses

Characters can obtain one or more of the following abilities during the raid.
Faction Bonus: Pirates
Whenever a Pirate gains a stack of Profit, they dispel all debuffs on themselves and their next ability deals bonus True damage equal to 10% of their Max Health to target enemy on their next turn.
Faction Bonus: Jedi Vanguard
+50% Max Health and Max Protection. Whenever any turn ends, if a Jedi Vanguard has 3 or more buffs, they gain 5% Offense (stacking) for the rest of the encounter.
Faction Bonus: Dark Side Clone Troopers
Whenever an enemy is inflicted with Order 66, all Dark Side Clone Troopers and Grand Moff Tarkin gain 25% Turn Meter and 100% Offense for 1 turn.
Faction Bonus: Bad Batch
[Stealthed characters deal 5% more damage. Non-Stealthed characters take 5% less damage when targeted.
Hero Bonus: Jedi Master Mace Windu
Whenever an enemy is Revealed, Jedi Master Mace Windu dispels all debuffs on himself and recovers 100% Health. If Jedi Master Mace Windu is the ally in the Leader slot, Jedi Vanguards gain benefits based on their role:
- Attackers: +100% Offense
- Healers and Supports: Dispel Stealth at the start of their turn
- Tanks: Gain Protection Up (100%) for 2 turns whenever Jedi Master Mace Windu uses a Special ability
Hero Bonus: Jocasta Nu
Whenever a Jedi Vanguard ally attacks out of turn, Jocasta's cooldowns are reduced by 1. Whenever she uses a Special ability Jedi Vanguard allies gain Crit Avoidance Up for 2 turns.
Hero Bonus: Kelleran Beq
While Kelleran Beq has full Health he has +25% Speed.
Hero Bonus: Grand Moff Tarkin
Grand Moff Tarkin gains Tenacity equal to 5% of his Potency.
Hero Bonus: CC-1119 "Appo"
While Knightfall Trooper is active, Appo has +25% Defense.
Hero Bonus: Omega (Fugitive)
Whenever Omega dispels a debuff, she gains 50% Tenacity for 1 turn.

Dire Fortitude

Dire Fortitude: +35% Critical Chance, Defense, and Offense; whenever this character gains a buff, they also gain 10% Critical Damage (stacking) for the rest of the battle
[Nom Capacité 1 (EN)]

Subdued: -35% Critical Avoidance, Defense, and Tenacity
Lord Vader
+
Vindictive Strike
Deal True damage to target enemy. Enemies defeated by this ability can't be revived.
My New Empire
Other Empire allies have +40 Speed, +40% Mastery, and +30% Max Health.

Whenever a Dark Side ally loses a buff, Empire allies gain 1% Mastery (stacking) until the end of battle. Whenever a debuffed Dark Side ally takes damage, they gain 20 Speed for 1 turn.

Empire allies have +35% Critical Chance and Critical Damage when targeting a Galactic Republic enemy. Whenever an enemy critically hits an Empire ally, that enemy has -30% Critical Damage (stacking) for 3 turns.

While Empire allies are at certain Health thresholds, they gain different benefits:
- While above 35% Health: Empire allies are immune to Turn Meter reduction
- While above 50% Health: They are immune to Damage Over Time and cooldown increase
- While below 80% Health: Can't be critically hit
Twisted Omenspan>
Lord Vader can't be targeted while all Knightfall Troopers or Disguised Clone Troopers are active. Lord Vader is immune to cooldown and Turn Meter manipulation and has 0 Speed. At the start of battle, Lord Vader has 25 stacks of Execute (reduced to 15 if there are fewer than 5 enemies).

Whenever a Dark Side Clone Trooper ally scores a critical hit, Lord Vader loses 1 stack of Execute. Whenever all stacks of Execute are lost, Lord Vader takes a bonus turn, which can't be prevented. After this bonus turn, he is reset to 25 stacks of Execute, and all defeated Disguised Clone Troopers and Knightfall Troopers are reinforced.

+

Defender of the Empire
Call a random Dark Side Clone Trooper Support ally to assist and they Stealth for 1 turn at the end of the round. Then deal Physical damage to target opponent. Appo and Knightfall Trooper gain Advantage for 1 turn.
Battalion Brothers

Disguised Clone Trooper
+
Covert Assault
Deal Special damage to target enemy. If Disguised Clone Trooper has Ruse, Stun the target enemy for 1 turn, which can't be resisted. If the target was already Stunned, dispel all buffs on them and inflict Order 66 for 2 turns, which can't be copied, dispelled, or resisted.
Well-Executed Ambush
Deal Special damage to all enemies, and deal an additional 10% damage per Damage Over Time stack on them (20% if Disguised Clone Trooper has Ruse, max 200% additional damage). Then grant Dark Side Clone Trooper allies Advantage for 2 turns.

If an enemy was:

- Less than full Health: Inflict Healing Immunity on them for 1 turn. If Disguised Clone Trooper has Ruse, Healing Immunity can't be dispelled or resisted, and inflict Damage Over Time for 2 turns.
- Less than 10% Turn Meter: Remove 10% Turn Meter then inflict -15 Speed on them for 2 turns. If Disguised Clone Trooper has Ruse, inflict an additional -15 Speed, and inflict Damage Over Time for 2 turns.
- Light Side: Inflict Offense Down on them for 2 turns. If Disguised Clone Trooper has Ruse, Offense Down can't be dispelled or resisted, and inflict Damage Over Time for 2 turns.
Clever Ruse
At the start of his turn, if Disguised Clone Trooper has Stealth, he recovers 15% Health and Protection, gains 20% Turn Meter, and gains Ruse until the end of the turn. Whenever he attacks, he loses Stealth.

At the start of each opponent's turn, if Disguised Clone Trooper has Stealth, ally Dark Side Clone Troopers gain Retribution until the end of the turn, if that opponent is a Jedi, Disguised Clone Trooper gains Tenacity Up until the end of that opponent's turn, which can't be copied, dispelled, or prevented.

Ruse: This effect triggers additional effects in abilities. Ruse is removed at the end of the turn.
Decoy Squad Five
At the start of battle, Disguised Clone Trooper gains 30% Offense and Stealth for 2 turns.

At the end of his turn, if Disguised Clone Trooper did not have Ruse, he Stealths for 3 turns.

If a target enemy had Order 66, Disguised Clone Trooper deals reduced Massive damage to them when using an ability on his turn.

+

No Witnesses
Deal Physical Damage and trigger all Damage Over Time debuffs on all enemies. Knightfall Trooper assists and gains Advantage for 2 turns. If Appo is not debuffed, inflict Order 66 on the target enemy for 2 turns, which can't be copied, dispelled, evaded, or resisted.

Dark Side Clone Troopers dispel Foresight on all opponents at the start of their turn. Whenever an enemy is defeated, reduce the cooldown of No Witnesses by 1. At the start of battle, Dark Side Clone Troopers gain Dramatic Entrance for 2 turns, which can't be copied, dispelled, or prevented. Whenever Appo uses a Special ability, reinforce all defeated Disguised Clone Troopers and Knightfall Troopers, which can't be prevented. Appo is immune to Stun.
